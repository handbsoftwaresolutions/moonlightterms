Privacy Policy

Moonlight
Effective date: September 15, 2026
Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pyncode AI F.Z.E and Handbsoftware Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Moonlight mobile application ("Moonlight" or the "App").

Contact: If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at support@moonlightmanifest.app.

By using Moonlight, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who We Are and What Moonlight Is

Moonlight is a personal manifestation and wellness app. It helps you create personalized manifestation stories and affirmations, save private Whisper posts, choose a narration voice, and (if you subscribe) unlock premium features.

Moonlight does not use a traditional email/password login. Your profile is tied to a device identifier and an onboarding profile ID created when you complete setup.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Onboarding and profile answers, which may include: name, date of birth / age, gender, sexual orientation, relationship-related answers, personal reflections, manifestation goals, and details you choose to share (including names or descriptions of people you are manifesting, if you enter them).
  • Profile photo (from camera or photo library).
  • Whisper posts you create (text and/or photos). Whisper content is private to your account and is not shown as a public feed to other users in the App.
  • Optional profile notes / feedback you submit (for example, "Anything Moonlight should know?").
  • Story prompts and related inputs you enter when generating manifestation stories.
  • Narration voice preference (which voice you select for story playback). We do not record your microphone for this feature.
  • Language and similar preference settings you choose in the App.
  • Support messages you send us by email.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device ID and related identifiers used to operate your account without a password login.
  • Onboarding / profile ID associated with your account on our servers.
  • Technical and usage data such as app version, device type / OS, crash diagnostics, and approximate usage analytics.
  • Push notification token and timezone (when notifications are enabled), so we can deliver reminders and engagement notifications.
  • IP address and standard server logs recorded by our hosting infrastructure (Vercel) when you connect to our services.

2.3 Purchase information

If you subscribe or make an in-app purchase, payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. We may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and related entitlement information needed to unlock premium features.

2.4 Permissions

With your permission, the App may access:

  • Camera — to take a profile photo or Whisper photo.
  • Photo library — to choose a profile or Whisper photo, and to save images you choose to save (for example when sharing or saving a Whisper image).
  • Notifications — to send push notifications if you allow them.

We do not request microphone access for narration voice selection.

3. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you during onboarding, profile edits, story creation, Whisper posts, feedback, and settings.
  • Through device permissions you grant (camera, photos, notifications).
  • Automatically through the App and our backend API (hosted on Vercel).
  • From Apple, Google, and RevenueCat in connection with purchases and subscription status.
  • From Google Firebase services for messaging, analytics, and crash reporting.
  • From Cloudinary, where user-uploaded images are stored.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and operate your Moonlight profile.
  • Personalize manifestation stories, affirmations, and related content.
  • Generate AI-assisted stories and content based on your prompt and personalization data (see Section 5).
  • Store and display your private Whisper posts and profile photo.
  • Save and apply your narration voice preference.
  • Process subscriptions and unlock premium features.
  • Send push notifications you opt into (and respect your in-app notification preferences).
  • Analyze usage and improve the App (Firebase Analytics).
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Provide customer support and respond to deletion or data requests.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services and comply with legal obligations.

AI-generated content is for personal reflection and entertainment. It is not medical, mental-health, legal, or professional advice.

5. Artificial Intelligence

Some features (including manifestation / story creation) use artificial intelligence.

When you generate a story, we may send to our AI processing systems categories of data such as:

  • your prompt;
  • personalization information from your onboarding and profile answers; and
  • profile feedback you have shared,

so the story can be tailored to you.

Our AI provider is Google Gemini. Processing is performed to provide the feature you requested. If this provider changes in the future, we will update this section to stay accurate.

We do not use Whisper posts as a public social network. Whisper remains private to your account unless you choose to share content yourself through the device share sheet or third-party apps.

6. Third-Party Services

We use trusted service providers to operate Moonlight. They process data only as needed to provide their services to us (and subject to their own terms and policies where applicable):

  • Backend hosting / API: Vercel
  • Image hosting / CDN: Cloudinary (for user-uploaded images); some images are bundled locally within the App and are not transmitted anywhere
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — distribution, payments, and (on relevant platforms) push delivery
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management
  • Google Firebase — Cloud Messaging (push), Analytics, and Crashlytics
  • Google Gemini — AI story and content generation
  • Social / sharing tools — when you choose to share (for example Instagram, X/Twitter, or other apps). Data you share with those platforms is governed by their own policies.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. When We Share Information

  • With the processors listed above, to run the App.
  • With Apple or Google as required for purchases and platform services.
  • When you explicitly share content to another app or platform.
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where permitted by law, with notice where required.

We do not share your private Whisper posts with other Moonlight users as a public feed.

8. Data Storage and Security

  • On your device, identifiers and certain local data may be stored using secure storage mechanisms (such as iOS Keychain / Android Keystore-backed storage) and local app storage.
  • On our servers (hosted on Vercel, default region), profile, Whisper, feedback, notification, and related data are stored to provide the service.
  • User-uploaded images are stored via Cloudinary.
  • We use HTTPS for network communication and aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide Moonlight, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify associated profile and content data from our active systems promptly, and in any case within 30 days, except where we must retain limited information for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons, or in backups for a short additional period before those backups rotate.

Deleting your Moonlight account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. You must cancel subscriptions separately in your Apple or Google account settings. Charges may continue until you cancel with the store.

10. Your Choices and Rights

In the App you can:

  • Update certain profile information.
  • Control notification preferences (where available).
  • Delete Whisper posts you created.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete Account.

Account deletion is intended to remove your Moonlight profile and associated data we hold on our systems (including related Whisper content tied to that profile), and to clear local account-related data on the device where deletion is completed, subject to the retention limits above.

You may also contact us at support@moonlightmanifest.app to:

  • request access to personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • ask questions about our practices.

Depending on where you live (for example EU/UK GDPR or California CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to certain processing, restrict processing, data portability, or appeal a denial. To exercise these rights, email support@moonlightmanifest.app. We may need to verify your request (for example using device/onboarding identifiers or other information that helps us locate your account).

If you are in the EEA/UK, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Because Moonlight is identifier-based (device / onboarding ID) rather than email login, please include any details you have that help us find your account when you contact us.

11. Children's Privacy

Moonlight is not directed to children under 13. You must be at least 13 years old to use the App (as enforced during date-of-birth checks in onboarding).

Some questions and content involve personal topics (such as relationships and identity). If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 provided information, contact us at support@moonlightmanifest.app and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. International Transfers

We process and store information primarily in the United States (Vercel's default hosting region) and other countries where our providers operate. Those countries may have different data-protection laws than your home country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

13. Third-Party Links and Shared Content
